## Tasks

1. Create a Harold Edgerton-like "multiple copies" photograph of a moving scene. Crucial to this project will be reading in a video using MATLAB, Python or some other tool. `edgerton.py` reads a video with OpenCV and composites the frames; edit it to suit your scene. Go for styles like [Edgerton at the Michael Hoppen Gallery](https://www.michaelhoppengallery.com/artists/54-dr-harold-edgerton/overview/#/artworks/9778).

2. Open your favorite AI chat. Give it the original video, and ask it to create an image like the one you did in part 1.

3. Using your favorite AI chat, give it the best Edgerton-like photograph you could create from parts 1 and 2, and ask it to back out the video. Create a GIF from the frames it gives you.

## Submission

Submit **one PDF** named `Lab01.pdf`, the two-digit lab number. It shows (a) your best Edgerton photo from your code, or AI-generated code, (b) the best Edgerton photo that the AI directly creates from the video, and (c) frames of the best GIF the AI can back out from the photograph. Every red underlined placeholder must be filled in.

Tell your agent `Build the PDF.` It collects your results into the report, drafts the AI disclosure for your group to confirm, points out anything missing, and produces the ready-to-submit PDF. Commit the result images and the GIF, not the video.
